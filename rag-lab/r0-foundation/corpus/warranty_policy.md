# Meridian Appliances, Warranty Policy

Meridian provides a limited warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials
and workmanship.

Coverage periods by division:
- Kitchen appliances, including the DX500 dishwasher and the FR900 SmartFridge:
  two year limited warranty.
- Laundry appliances, including the WX200 washer: one year limited warranty.
- Climate appliances, including the AC300 QuietAC: one year limited warranty.

What the warranty covers: parts and labor to repair a confirmed manufacturing
defect during the coverage period. There is no service fee for a valid warranty
claim.

What the warranty excludes: damage from misuse, improper installation, cosmetic
wear, and consumable parts such as filters. The warranty applies to the original
purchaser.
