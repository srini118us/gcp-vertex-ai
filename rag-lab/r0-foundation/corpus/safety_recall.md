# Meridian Appliances, Safety Recall Notice

Meridian has issued a safety recall affecting the AC300 QuietAC.

Scope: AC300 units manufactured before the current production revision. No other
Meridian product is affected by this recall.

Cause: a defective compressor supplied by CoolCore can overheat under sustained
load.

Remedy: owners of an affected AC300 may choose a full refund or a free
replacement unit. Recalled AC300 units are exempt from the fifteen percent
restocking fee that normally applies to returns.

Owners should stop using an affected unit and contact Meridian support to arrange
the refund or replacement.
