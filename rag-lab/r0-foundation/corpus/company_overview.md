# Meridian Appliances, Company Overview

Meridian Appliances designs and sells home appliances across three divisions.

Divisions and products:
- Kitchen division: DX500 Pro Dishwasher, FR900 SmartFridge. Led by Sam Okafor.
- Laundry division: WX200 Compact Washer. Led by Priya Nair.
- Climate division: AC300 QuietAC. Led by Luis Mendez.

Company leadership: Dana Reyes is the chief executive. The three division leads
report to Dana Reyes.

Key component suppliers:
- Volt Components supplies the electronic control boards used in the DX500 and the
  FR900.
- Hydro Dynamics supplies the water pumps used in the DX500 and the WX200.
- CoolCore supplies the compressors used in the FR900 and the AC300.
