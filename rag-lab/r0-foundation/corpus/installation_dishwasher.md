# DX500 Pro Dishwasher, Installation and Troubleshooting

## Installation
1. Shut off the water supply and the circuit at the breaker.
2. Connect the inlet hose to the hot water valve and hand tighten.
3. Connect the drain hose to the disposal or drain tailpiece.
4. Level the unit with the adjustable feet and secure the mounting bracket.
5. Restore water and power, then run a rinse cycle to check for leaks.

## Troubleshooting
- If the unit does not power on, confirm the circuit breaker is set and that the
  door latch is fully engaged. The control board will not energize with the door
  unlatched.
- If the unit does not drain, check the drain hose for kinks and clear the sump
  filter.
- If dishes are not clean, verify the hot water supply reaches the recommended
  temperature and that the spray arms turn freely.

For defects during the warranty period, see the warranty policy.
