# Meridian Appliances, Returns and Refunds

Meridian accepts returns within a thirty day return window measured from the
delivery date.

Conditions:
- The item must be in original packaging and in resalable condition.
- A restocking fee of fifteen percent of the list price applies to returned
  appliances, unless the unit is defective or covered by a recall.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within ten business days of
  the returned item arriving at a Meridian depot.

A return is separate from a warranty claim. A warranty claim repairs a defect and
carries no fee. A return sends the product back for a refund and carries the
restocking fee unless an exception applies.
