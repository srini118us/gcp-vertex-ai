# Meridian Appliances, Support FAQ

Common customer questions handled by the support team.

Q: How do I register a Meridian product?
A: Register online with the model number and the purchase date to activate the
warranty record.

Q: What are support hours?
A: Support is available weekdays, nine to six, in the customer local time zone.

Q: Where is the model number located?
A: On the rating label, inside the door on kitchen appliances and on the rear
panel on climate appliances.

Q: Can I buy replacement filters?
A: Yes, consumable parts such as filters are sold separately and are not covered
by the warranty.
